The evidence gauge

Every claim carries its tier

No cherry-picking, no certainty theater. Each claim discussed on this ship is visibly classified:

Strong evidence

Replicated, consistent findings — close to current scientific consensus.

Promising

An early signal from small or few studies. Interesting, not settled.

Mixed

Results disagree. Context, dose of caution, and confounders matter.

Unsupported

The claim outruns the data it stands on.

Unknown

An honest blank — nobody has measured it well yet.

A tier describes the evidence — never a promise about you, and never a recommendation.

The observation log

A tracker that doesn't exist yet — on purpose

The long-term mission is a privacy-respecting observation log: a simple way to record routines, context and outcomes one variable at a time. It is not built, and it will never prescribe, dose, diagnose or sell your data. Join the waitlist to follow the build and shape what a trustworthy tracker would need.
