# Akiko / Being Tea Co. — Current Platform and Policy Intelligence

**Research snapshot:** August 9, 2026  
**Scope:** Instagram, Facebook, and X. Official platform sources are preferred. Product availability can vary by account, country, and rollout cohort, so every feature must still be confirmed inside the correct owner-controlled account before use.

## Operating conclusions

1. Akiko should use **Instagram as the primary discovery and visual-identity channel**, Facebook as the depth/community/qualified-view channel, and X as the concise conversation, correction, and link-distribution channel.
2. A public professional Instagram account should be checked in **Account Status before launch and weekly thereafter**. Recommendation eligibility permits possible non-follower distribution; it does not guarantee it.
3. The launch still-image set is useful for identity validation, but sustained discovery will require original short-form video, carousels, and continuing series—not a static-image-only feed.
4. Facebook is explicitly rewarding original creators and deprioritizing duplicative or minimally edited media. Platform-specific shots, edits, hooks, captions, and uploads are therefore a creative and distribution requirement, not cosmetic busywork.
5. Every photorealistic Akiko asset should retain plain-language fictional/AI disclosure, use Meta's native AI disclosure control when presented, and never imply that the pictured scene documents a real tasting, visit, farmer, garden, ceremony, or result.
6. Monetization should not drive the launch. First prove qualified attention, repeat viewing, profile conversion, newsletter conversion, and trust.

## Instagram

### Current official guidance

- Public-account content may be recommended to non-followers in Explore, Reels, feed recommendations, search, and suggested accounts when eligible. Professional accounts can inspect recommendation eligibility, profile issues, removed content, and feature restrictions in Account Status. Eligibility is not a promise of distribution. [Recommendation eligibility](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/653964212890722) and [Account Status](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/338481628002750/).
- Instagram's **Best Practices** area in the professional dashboard provides account-specific guidance across creation, engagement, reach, monetization, and guidelines. Treat its personalized recommendations as inputs to experiments, not universal truth. [Instagram Best Practices announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/).
- **Trial Reels** can show a Reel to non-followers first, surface early metrics after roughly 24 hours, and optionally share a successful trial more broadly. The account must confirm the toggle is available before relying on it. [Trial Reels](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/).
- Instagram keeps photos at original resolution up to 1080 pixels wide when the aspect ratio is between 1.91:1 and 3:4. The v2 Instagram launch exports use 1080×1440, the supported 3:4 maximum portrait ratio. [Instagram photo resolution](https://www.facebook.com/help/1631821640426723/).
- Reels may be uploaded between 1.91:1 and 9:16, with at least 720-pixel resolution and 30 FPS. Instagram's current help says Reels over three minutes are not recommended to new audiences. Use 9:16 and test 12–25, 25–45, and 45–90 seconds before attempting longer education. [Reel specifications](https://www.facebook.com/help/1038071743007909) and [recording Reels](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/225190788256708).
- Monetizable content must be original or meaningfully authored. Misleading medical information and unoriginal content are ineligible. [Instagram Content Monetization Policies](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/2635536099905516).
- Meta may display **AI info** when it detects generation signals or when an uploader self-discloses. Akiko's caption disclosure remains necessary for audience clarity even if a native label appears. [Meta AI-content labeling](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/).

### Akiko's first-30-day Instagram experiments

| Experiment | Single variable | Primary metric | Decision rule after a useful sample |
|---|---|---|---|
| Static portrait vs teaching carousel | Format | Follows per 1,000 qualified views | Keep the format with materially higher median follow conversion after at least 4 posts per cell. |
| Direct question vs sensory observation hook | Opening line | Initial carousel advancement or Reel hold | Scale only if the seven-day median improves without lower saves or more negative feedback. |
| 15–25s vs 35–50s Reel | Duration | Watch-time-to-duration ratio and follows/1,000 | Retain both if they serve different outcomes; stop a cell after 4 comparable posts if consistently inferior. |
| Trial Reel vs normal Reel | Distribution setting | Non-follower qualified reach | Use Trial only when available and when it increases learning without hiding a proven franchise from followers. |
| Face-led cover vs process-led cover | Cover subject | Profile visits/1,000 | Choose by series; do not force Akiko's face when the leaf or technique produces better qualified behavior. |

### Weekly account checks

- Account Status: recommendation eligibility, profile issues, removed content, feature limitations.
- Best Practices dashboard: record suggestions, but change only one controlled variable at a time.
- Non-follower reach, search terms, saves, shares, profile visits, follows attributed to content, returning viewers, and unfollows.
- AI disclosure control visibility and caption disclosure presence.
- Audio licenses and removal of third-party watermarks.

## Facebook

### Current official guidance

- Facebook announced that its video creation flow is moving all new video uploads into Reels, without a separate video-versus-Reel choice and without Reel length or format restrictions. Native video still needs a deliberate opening and platform-specific context. [Facebook video creation update](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/making-it-easier-create-videos-facebook/).
- Facebook states that content produced by the Page/Profile owner is original, while reuploads, minor edits, low-value reactions, and duplicative content may be deprioritized. Akiko's Facebook exports therefore need their own framing and should not be identical watermarked Instagram files. [Rewarding original creators](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/).
- Facebook's 2026 creator update says Content Monetization can cover eligible Reels, Stories, photos, and text posts. The program remains invite-only, with an interest form in the Professional Dashboard. New metrics include qualified views, approximate earnings per 1,000 qualified views, and non-qualified-view reasons. [Creator Fast Track and monetization metrics](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/).
- Creator Assistant is rolling out in the United States, Canada, and India with account-specific idea and performance guidance. If present, record its explanation beside—not instead of—the raw metrics and Akiko experiment register. [Facebook Creator Assistant](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/creator-assistant-more-languages-for-ai-translations-on-facebook/).
- Facebook says its recommendation system is surfacing more fresh/same-day Reels and that longer-than-one-minute Reels account for substantial watch time among established creators. This supports testing both concise and deeper lessons; it is not evidence that a new Akiko account should default to long video. [Facebook Reels recommendations](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/10/finding-sharing-reels-facebook-just-got-easier-more-fun/).
- Account Status covers the profile, managed Pages, Community Standards restrictions, monetization limits, and recommendations status. [Facebook Account Status](https://www.facebook.com/help/1392616391875085).
- Facebook recommends supported photo formats and files under 15 MB. The v2 exports use sRGB JPEG at 1080×1350 for a mobile-first 4:5 working composition; preview every crop because Facebook does not prescribe that as a universal organic-feed ratio. [Facebook photo guidance](https://www.facebook.com/help/121317464722113).

### Akiko's first-30-day Facebook experiments

| Experiment | Single variable | Primary metric | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 180–300 word teaching caption vs 70–120 word summary | Caption depth | Meaningful comments and outbound clicks/1,000 | Keep deeper copy when it adds qualified discussion or clicks without sharply reducing consumption. |
| Native photo post vs native 35–60s Reel | Format | Qualified views and follows/1,000 | Scale the repeatable median winner; retain photo posts if they drive saves, link clicks, or substantive comments. |
| Source link in post vs first human comment | Link placement | Outbound clicks and qualified reach | Use only owner-controlled manual posting and measure both cells; do not hide commercial relationships. |
| Beginner question vs experienced-drinker question | Audience framing | Meaningful comment rate | Choose the framing that attracts the intended audience rather than the largest raw comment count. |
| 45–75s vs 90–150s native video | Duration | Qualified views and average watch time | Require 4 comparable posts per cell; length wins only if retention and follows stay healthy. |

### Weekly account checks

- Qualified versus non-qualified views if the metrics are available.
- Originality/copyright warnings and impersonation alerts.
- Shares into private conversations or Groups where visible; do not solicit or automate Group posting.

## X

### Current official guidance

- A single image with an aspect ratio between 2:1 and 3:4 displays in full. Photos may be JPEG, PNG, or GIF up to 5 MB. Akiko's X exports use 1600×900 JPEG and must include native alt text. [X image guidance](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/posting-gifs-and-pictures).
- X prohibits platform manipulation, follow churn, purchased/coordinated engagement, duplicate or near-duplicate bulk posts, unsolicited bulk replies/DMs, and deceptive fake-persona behavior. A fictional identity can operate only when its source and nature are transparent and it is not impersonating or disrupting others. [X Authenticity policy](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/authenticity).
- X's recommendation guidance says recently violating or spam-associated accounts may be ineligible for recommendations. [X recommendations](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/recommendations).
- Automation must use the official API and may not script the website, evade limits, send spam, or automate unsolicited mentions/replies. Akiko remains manual/approval-controlled. [X automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation).
- Creator Revenue Sharing currently requires an active eligible Premium subscription, at least five million organic impressions in the prior three months, at least 500 verified followers, a supported country, and rule compliance. It is not an early launch objective. [X Creator Revenue Sharing](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/creator-revenue-sharing).
- X Subscriptions currently lists at least 2,000 verified followers, five million organic impressions in the prior three months, age 18+, and recent activity among its minimum application requirements. [X Creator Subscriptions](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/subscriptions-creator).

### Akiko's first-30-day X experiments

| Experiment | Single variable | Primary metric | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single distilled insight vs 3-post mini-thread | Structure | Profile visits and follows/1,000 impressions | Keep threads only when continuation adds qualified actions rather than empty impressions. |
| Image-led vs text-only | Media | Qualified engagement and link clicks | Use the median of at least 5 comparable posts per cell. |
| Definition hook vs controlled-experiment hook | Hook | Detail expands, replies, and saves/bookmarks where available | Scale the hook that attracts tea questions, not generic arguments. |
| Site link vs no link | Destination | Link clicks and follows/1,000 | Use links when the destination is the point; do not turn the feed into repetitive link drops. |
| Morning vs early evening Alaska time | Time | Qualified impressions in first 24h | Run paired weekday tests before setting a default window. |

### Weekly account checks

- Write scope and official connector status before any scheduling attempt.
- Account authenticity and recommendation eligibility signals.
- Duplicate-content review across Akiko and the owner's other personas.
- Impressions, profile visits, follows, link clicks, meaningful replies, bookmarks, and unfollows.
- No automated replies, arguments, likes, follows, or unsolicited DMs.

## Platform operating playbooks

### Instagram operating playbook

- **Funnel job:** non-follower discovery → save/profile visit → follow → Start Here/newsletter.
- **Native formats:** 3:4 single images for the launch identity; saveable carousels for terms and comparisons; 9:16 Reels for brewing motion, controlled tests, and questions; Stories for low-stakes follow-up after a real audience exists.
- **Search/discovery:** write the literal tea/topic term in the name field, opening caption, and spoken/on-screen teaching where natural; use accurate alt text for accessibility rather than keyword stuffing; review actual search terms and non-follower reach weekly. Never disguise unrelated trend terms as tea content.
- **Community behavior:** answer specific tea questions, turn repeated questions into posts, and use two bounded human-reviewed reply windows. Do not mass reply, auto-DM, or argue.
- **Collaboration features:** use Instagram Collab posts only with a verified contributor who accepts co-authorship and approves attribution; accepted posts can appear on both profiles and be distributed to both audiences. Manage remix permissions deliberately and obtain written reuse rights outside the native remix context. [Instagram Collab posts](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/291200585956732) and [Instagram remix controls](https://www.facebook.com/help/792438641600810/).
- **Analytics:** non-follower reach, initial hold/advancement, watch-time ratio, completion, saves, shares, profile visits, follows attributed, returning viewers, search terms, unfollows, and negative feedback.
- **Monetization role:** later-stage sponsorship proof, disclosed affiliate/product discovery, and traffic to owned education. Do not optimize launch content for monetization eligibility.
- **Do not copy directly:** Facebook-length explanations, X screenshot cards, watermarked Reels, or an identical cover/crop from another platform.

### Facebook operating playbook

- **Funnel job:** deeper explanation and discussion → source/link click → newsletter/owned guide; native qualified video can become a secondary discovery engine.
- **Native formats:** 4:5 mobile-first photos, native Reels/video, text/photo explainers, and owner-reviewed Page posts with sources.
- **Search/discovery:** use clear topic language such as “tea vs tisane,” “water for tea,” or “oolong leaf” in the first lines and page metadata; earn sharing through useful explanation rather than soliciting Group distribution. Track qualified/non-qualified views and search/discovery fields when exposed.
- **Community behavior:** invite specific experience reports, preserve source links, and moderate for substance. Do not automate Group posting or copy/paste the same reply across unrelated threads.
- **Collaboration features:** an authorized Page creator can add a collaborator when creating a Page post; public Reels may also be remixed when the owner permits it. Use only with contributor approval, clear source ownership, and saved rights records. [Facebook Page posts and collaborators](https://www.facebook.com/help/642707099175171/) and [Facebook Reel remix](https://www.facebook.com/help/iphone-app/526191278369056).
- **Analytics:** qualified and non-qualified views, average watch time, retention, meaningful comments, shares, negative feedback, Page follows, link clicks, newsletter signups, and originality/copyright notices.
- **Monetization role:** eventual Content Monetization if invited, plus transparent site/newsletter traffic and sponsorship depth. Invitation or eligibility must never be implied before the dashboard confirms it.
- **Do not copy directly:** an Instagram download, Instagram-short caption when context is needed, or an X-style assertion without evidence and boundaries.

### X operating playbook

- **Funnel job:** searchable distinctions and timely conversation → profile visit → source/site click; use the channel as a public correction and question-sensing layer.
- **Native formats:** concise standalone posts, short threads only when continuation adds value, 16:9 images with alt text, source links, polls only for genuine research questions, and human-operated Spaces only after approval.
- **Search/discovery:** lead with the literal term or problem, keep one post focused on one claim, use Lists to monitor named tea educators/sources, and join relevant Communities only as a genuine participant. X says Lists organize topic/account streams and Communities are visible but member participation is rule-governed. [X Lists](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-lists) and [X Communities](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/communities).
- **Community behavior:** answer good-faith questions, correct without quote-amplifying low-reach misinformation, and never run automated arguments, mentions, follow churn, or unsolicited DMs.
- **Collaboration features:** use public replies/quote posts carefully and, only after a human host/moderator plan is approved, a Space with a qualified guest. X allows up to two co-hosts and makes Spaces public; the host is responsible for moderation. [X Spaces](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/spaces).
- **Analytics:** impressions, profile visits, follows, link clicks, meaningful replies, bookmarks where available, detail expands where available, unfollows, and negative feedback/account restrictions.
- **Monetization role:** no launch role. Revenue Sharing or Subscriptions remain future eligibility paths, not reasons to buy Premium or chase low-quality impressions.
- **Do not copy directly:** long Facebook prose, an Instagram carousel flattened into screenshots, repeated link drops, or near-duplicate posts across owner personas.

## YouTube decision

The recorded `youtube.com/@BeingTeaCo` surface was unavailable in the prior audit while two legacy Being Tea Co. videos remained reachable. Do not create a replacement handle, file a recovery claim, or upload until the owner verifies account ownership and recovery options through official Google/YouTube flows.

Decision path:

1. **Recover** if the owner can prove control and the channel history is useful.
2. **Replace** only with an owner-authorized new Brand Account and a documented redirect/archive plan.
3. **Retire from the 90-day launch** if neither can be verified quickly; preserve the legacy links as historical evidence and focus production on Instagram, Facebook, X, and the owned site.

Working recommendation: **retire YouTube from the launch scope pending an owner-led recovery check**, then reconsider once one short-form franchise is proven. No YouTube account action was attempted.

## Native-repurposing rule

Each concept can share a lesson, production session, and identity anchor, but it must be authored three times:

| Source idea | Instagram | Facebook | X |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core lesson | Visual discovery and saveable practice | Full explanation, evidence, boundaries, discussion | One useful distinction or compact thread |
| Image/video | 3:4 photo, carousel, or 9:16 Reel | 4:5 photo or native Reel with more context | 16:9 image or concise native video |
| Hook | Immediate visual tension | Concrete problem plus context | TL;DR assertion/question |
| CTA | Save, compare, or visit profile | Discuss experience or read the source | Open source/link or answer one precise question |
| File | Clean native export | Clean native export, not an IG download | Clean native export, not a Meta download |

- Recheck official platform pages immediately before the first publish action.
- Recheck monetization requirements monthly and whenever an in-app eligibility panel appears.
- Recheck recommendation/account status weekly.
- Record feature availability by account; do not claim a feature exists merely because Meta or X announced a rollout.
