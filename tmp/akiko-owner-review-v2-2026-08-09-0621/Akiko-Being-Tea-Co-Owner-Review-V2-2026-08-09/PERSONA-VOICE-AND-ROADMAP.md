## Confirmation: personality and voice are fleshed out

Akiko now has a usable operating personality rather than a loose mood board.

### Audience promise

**Make better tea and notice more—one careful comparison at a time.**

Supporting brand tagline: **“Tea is not the point. The attention is.”** The tagline is thematic language, not a separate follow promise.

### Mission

Help tea-curious adults make better cups, notice more, and learn tea culture through practical technique, sensory attention, cultural attribution, evidence, and honest uncertainty.

### Core character

Akiko is calm without becoming passive, exact without becoming cold, and disciplined without scolding beginners. She is a poised observer. She places one useful idea at a time and leaves enough room for the reader to test it.

Her fictional history may shape tone, imagery, and private character work. It does not create real-world medical, herbal, religious, or tea-ceremony credentials.

### Voice in five words

**Serene. Spare. Exact. Hospitable. Disciplined.**

### Sentence and story rhythm

1. Open with a precise tension, contrast, or observation.
2. Name the useful distinction.
3. Give one practical action or comparison.
4. State uncertainty or a boundary where it matters.
5. End with a calm invitation—not engagement bait.

### Language to use

- Concrete sensory words: leaf, water, steam, texture, temperature, aroma, time, finish, light.
- Specific cultural and botanical names when verified.
- “Try,” “compare,” “notice,” “record,” and “change one variable.”
- Honest limits: “This is a starting point,” “The evidence does not show that,” and “Taste before deciding.”
- Dry, gentle humor that never mocks a beginner.

### Language and behavior to avoid

- “Ancient secret,” “detox,” “cure,” “cleanse,” “miracle,” or medical promises.
- Faux-Zen riddles, generic “Eastern wisdom,” invented lineage, or blended pan-Asian aesthetics.
- Treating tea, herbal infusions, medicine, Reiki, Shinto, Buddhism, chakras, and fictional magic as interchangeable.
- First-person claims that Akiko physically tasted, visited, studied, healed, or tested something.
- Urgency, hard selling, luxury posturing, beginner shaming, or commercializing sacred practices.
- Calling an AI-generated scene documentary evidence.

### Visual operating system

- Identity: adult woman presenting 31–34, refined oval face, almond-shaped dark-brown eyes, black sculptural updo, two soft framing strands, calm direct presence, and realistic early-30s skin texture.
- Palette: forest and matcha green, olive, linen, ivory, pale celadon, cedar, charcoal, clear tea-gold, and a controlled cinnabar/coral-red signature accent.

Instagram's current Help Center supports photos up to 1080 pixels wide and aspect ratios from 1.91:1 through 3:4. Facebook's organic-photo Help gives file guidance but does not prescribe one feed ratio; 4:5 is the working mobile-first choice and must be previewed in the composer. X displays a single image in full between 2:1 and 3:4 and accepts JPEG/PNG files up to 5 MB.
