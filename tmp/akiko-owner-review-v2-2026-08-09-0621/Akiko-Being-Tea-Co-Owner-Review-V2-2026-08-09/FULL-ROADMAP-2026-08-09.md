## 1. Honest executive diagnosis

### Strongest strategic advantage

Being Tea Co. already has a documented education-first history, an existing article library, brewing guides, botanical material, Between Steeps work, and a deliberate correction/disclosure posture. Akiko adds a stable visual host and emotional continuity without requiring the founder to become an always-on on-camera creator.

### Biggest obstacle

The account starts without a proven audience, follow-conversion baseline, regular human tasting footage, public authority, current analytics, or functioning publishing authorization. Photorealistic AI images can attract attention, but they are not proof of expertise or experience and can erode trust if the character becomes more prominent than the useful work.

### Clearest audience wedge

Tea-curious adults who want to make better cups and understand what they are drinking but feel alienated by jargon, expensive gear, unsupported wellness promises, status competition, and vague mysticism.

### Distinctness diagnosis

“Calm tea content” is not distinct. The usable promise is:

> **One careful tea distinction or controlled home experiment at a time, hosted by a clearly disclosed fictional character who never borrows lived authority.**

That is sufficiently distinct to test. It is not yet proven by audience behavior.

### Missing proof, assets, skills, or access

- Current Facebook Page URL and follower count.
- Fresh platform analytics and website/newsletter baseline.
- Meta authorization and correct Page–Instagram linkage.
- X write scope through an official connector if scheduling is desired.
- Human-run tasting footage and measured results for any claim about a specific tea or product.
- Production-capacity ceiling, monthly budget, sponsorship exclusions, and named global-pause owner.
- Final rights confirmation for every visual reference and future music/audio.
- Evidence that a fictional AI host improves qualified follow behavior rather than merely generating curiosity.

### Healthy success versus the stretch outcome

| Outcome | What it would mean by December 31 |
|---|---|
| Healthy foundation | One repeatable franchise, consistent native publishing, no trust/policy incidents, 10,000 combined real followers, 500+ opted-in email readers, and evidence of follow conversion from at least two series. |
| Strong outcome | 25,000–100,000 combined followers, several repeatable discovery posts, an owned-audience conversion path, and reliable weekly production. |
| Breakout outcome | 250,000+ combined followers driven by several repeatable franchises and major earned distribution—not one isolated viral post. |
| One-million stretch | Repeated cross-platform breakout reach, unusually high qualified follow conversion, fast collaboration access, sustained output, and no policy/auth interruption. It remains possible in the mathematical sense and low-confidence in the evidence sense. |

### Most important decision first

Before anything goes live, align the public profiles around one plain identity statement: **“Fictional, AI-generated editorial host. Human-directed Being Tea Co. account.”** A profile that looks human but hides the operating model creates more risk than any content tactic can repair.

### Qualified-reach requirement

The actual requirement cannot be calculated until follows attributable to qualified views are available. Use:

`required qualified views = remaining follower gap ÷ observed follow conversion per qualified view`

Planning sensitivities—not universal benchmarks:

### Weekly reforecast rules

Every Monday after launch:

1. Replace the baseline floor with verified platform totals.
2. Use rolling seven- and 28-day medians for qualified reach, follows/1,000, and production time.
3. Calculate remaining gap and required daily/weekly net follows.
4. Classify the stretch as **on mathematical line**, **requires acceleration**, or **no longer evidence-supported**.
5. Change the public operating target only after the owner sees both the healthy and stretch forecasts.
6. Do not extend a failed format because one post went viral; require a repeat or a clear mechanism.
7. If no series beats the account median after four comparable episodes, revise or stop it.

## 3. Brand operating system

### Positioning statement

For tea-curious adults overwhelmed by ritual, jargon, wellness claims, and shopping advice, Akiko is Being Tea Co.'s clearly disclosed fictional editorial host who turns tea into one calm, sourced observation or controlled experiment at a time. Unlike vendor-first or mysticism-first accounts, Akiko makes uncertainty and provenance visible.

### Follow promise

**Follow Akiko to make better tea and notice more—one careful comparison at a time.**

### Profile system

**Display name:** `Being Tea Co. | Akiko`  
**Handle:** retain the existing Being Tea Co. handle where possible; do not rename accounts until ownership, cross-platform consistency, and redirect implications are checked.  
**Bio:**

> Tea education, one careful comparison at a time.  
> Akiko is a fictional, AI-generated host. Human-directed.  
> Start here ↓

**Link strategy:** one owner-controlled landing page with four choices only: Start Here, Brewing Guides, Between Steeps, Newsletter. Do not use an affiliate aggregator until there are active, disclosed programs worth presenting.  
**Profile image:** close crop of the approved v2 portrait, face readable at small circular size, minimal ornament near crop edge, no text.  
**Instagram pinned sequence:** 1) Meet Akiko and disclosure, 2) Start Here: tea vs tisane / one plant, 3) Change One Variable.  
**Facebook featured/pinned:** brand introduction with full history/disclosure and Start Here link.  
**X pinned:** compact identity/disclosure plus a 3-post “start here” thread and site link.

### Welcome sequence

1. **Who this is:** fictional AI-generated host; human-directed account.
2. **What followers get:** practical tea distinctions and experiments.
3. **What Akiko does not claim:** tasting, travel, lineage, medicine, or ceremony credentials.
4. **First useful action:** brew the same tea twice while changing one variable.
5. **Owned step:** download/save the one-line brewing log or join the newsletter.

### Voice

**Serene, spare, exact, hospitable, disciplined.** Open with a precise observation; teach one distinction; give one action; name the limit; end with a calm invitation. Never perform wisdom or mock a beginner.

### Visual identity

- Adult presenting 31–34 with stable v2 face, almond-shaped dark eyes, sculptural black updo, framing strands, and realistic skin texture.
- Red floral brocade and restrained blossom/gold ornament are the rare ceremonial signature, especially the introduction.
- Everyday education uses forest, olive, charcoal, ivory, linen, celadon, cedar, and tea-gold.
- Natural side light, tactile leaf/water/ceramic detail, plausible hands and liquids, no baked-in text or logos.
- Calm is the treatment; **the concrete question is the hook**.

### Core pillars

1. **Tea fundamentals:** plant, processing, category, terminology, and equipment without gatekeeping.
2. **Controlled brewing:** change one variable and record what happens.
3. **Sensory attention:** look, smell, sip, feel, wait—framed as audience practice, not Akiko's bodily experience.
4. **Source and culture literacy:** origin, attribution, translation, evidence, and correction boundaries.
5. **Tea as conversation:** Between Steeps prompts, logs, questions, and community observations.

### What Akiko owns

- **Intellectually:** distinguish evidence, tradition, direct human experience, hypothesis, and experiment.
- **Emotionally:** permission to slow down without pretending, performing expertise, or buying status.

### What Akiko will never become

A fake human life, wellness oracle, ceremonial authority, generic “Asian wisdom” aesthetic, product-review puppet, rage-bait account, or mass-produced AI-image feed.

### Separation from other personas

- Castleborn lore remains private unless a future post is explicitly framed as fiction and separately approved.
- Brother Kāruṇya is not a co-host for Being Tea Co. during this launch.
- The founder owns Being Tea Co.'s real 2015 history and Deer Park/Plum Village experience; Akiko never speaks those experiences in first person.
- Other MyPersonas accounts must not repost identical Akiko content or coordinate engagement.

### Final stretch — Day 91 through December 31

- Allocate 70% of output to repeatable winners, 20% to adjacent tests, 10% to bold experiments.
- Use timely seasonal tea questions only when they fit the brand and can be sourced.
- Seek earned distribution through expert contributions, newsletters, podcasts, events, and communities—not spam replies.
- Maintain weekly reforecast and protect the healthy outcome even if the one-million stretch becomes unsupported.
