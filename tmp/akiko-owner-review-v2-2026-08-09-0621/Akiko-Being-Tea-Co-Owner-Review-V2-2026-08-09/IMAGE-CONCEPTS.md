# Akiko / Being Tea Co. — Ten Launch Image Concepts

Status: **Version-2 production brief executed for local owner review. All 30 v2 images exist; nothing here authorizes posting or scheduling.**

## Character and campaign invariants

- The completed v2 derivatives used `identity/akiko-canonical-portrait-v2.png` as the primary **identity reference**. Keep the private owner-supplied image as provenance/reference material only; do not publish it.
- Preserve the same adult fictional Japanese woman presenting 31–34: refined oval face, almond-shaped dark-brown eyes, straight black hair in a sculptural high updo with two soft framing strands, natural adult skin texture, and a calm direct presence.
- Use the cinnabar/coral-red floral brocade and restrained blossom-and-gold ornament for the introduction and rare ceremonial brand moments. Use simpler forest, olive, charcoal, ivory, linen, and pale-celadon layers for routine tea education.
- Photorealistic natural editorial photography with real skin and material texture, restrained depth of field, warm natural side light, forest/matcha green, celadon, linen, cedar, charcoal, pale tea-gold, and controlled cinnabar accents.
- Akiko is poised and attentive, never theatrical, seductive, guru-like, mystical, or commercially over-polished.
- Use culturally specific utensils only where they are appropriate to the scene. Do not turn one tradition's tools into a generic symbol for all tea.
- No text, captions, logos, packaging brands, labels, watermark, decorative pseudo-calligraphy, religious symbols, monastery claims, fantasy castle, weapons, medical objects, supernatural effects, glowing aura, or invented location markers.
- Do not depict a real tea garden, named maker, named region, historical event, or product test. These are clearly editorial concept images.
- Hands, fingers, teaware, leaf, steam, reflections, and liquid must be anatomically and physically plausible.
- Leave clean composition that can survive native platform UI cropping. Every final image receives a plain-language caption disclosure: “AI-generated image of the fictional character Akiko.”

## Platform treatments

### Instagram

- Final: **1080 × 1440 JPEG (3:4)**.
- Intimate vertical framing; closer portrait or tactile detail; subject/action centered with breathing room.
- Viewpoint should differ from Facebook and X for the same concept.

### Facebook

- Final: **1080 × 1350 JPEG (4:5)**.
- Medium editorial framing with more environmental or instructional context; often a three-quarter angle.
- Viewpoint should differ from Instagram and X for the same concept.

### X

- Final: **1600 × 900 JPEG (16:9)**, under 5 MB.
- Horizontal, immediate, high-clarity composition; use a wider side, table-level, or overhead perspective.
- Viewpoint should differ from Instagram and Facebook for the same concept.

## Concept 01 — Meet Akiko / The attention is the point

Akiko sits at a simple warm cedar tea table and looks calmly toward the viewer. This introduction alone uses the ceremonial signature: cinnabar/coral-red floral brocade, ivory collar, and restrained blossom-and-gold ornament. One unbranded ceramic cup, a small pot, a folded linen cloth, and a thin curl of steam. The room is quiet and contemporary, with no claimed institution or religious setting. This is a clear character-and-brand introduction, not a fashion portrait; the wardrobe establishes identity but must not overpower the tea setting.

## Concept 02 — One plant, many paths

Akiko studies one fresh *Camellia sinensis* branch beside six small unbranded dishes containing visibly different processed tea leaves: white, green, yellow, oolong, black, and dark tea. Keep the samples separated and natural. No printed labels; the caption does the teaching.

## Concept 03 — Tea or tisane?

Akiko arranges two clearly separate groups on a tea table: processed *Camellia sinensis* leaf with an amber cup, and distinct unblended examples of peppermint, rooibos, chamomile, and chaga. Nothing implies that all botanicals share one origin or medical effect.

## Concept 04 — Read the opened leaf

Akiko gently opens and studies a brewed oolong leaf beside a small pile of dry rolled oolong and an unbranded white porcelain gaiwan. Emphasize leaf edges, stem, bud, color, and water sheen. The image communicates observation, not a beauty standard.

## Concept 05 — Finding the golden light

Akiko observes a clear pale-gold infusion in thin white porcelain near a real window. A single natural glint appears at the liquor edge. No artificial aura, magic, halo, or universal color claim; the light must read as ordinary daylight.

## Concept 06 — The five-pass cup reading

Akiko gives one cup her full attention: looking at the liquor, noticing steam, preparing to sip, and keeping a blank field notebook nearby. The still should suggest the sequence look, smell, sip, feel, wait without printed words or a staged multi-exposure effect.

## Concept 07 — One leaf, three brews

Akiko compares three identical unmarked white cups containing subtly different infusions from the same leaf. A kettle, small scale, simple timer, and thermometer are present but show no readable figures. The scene is a controlled comparison, not a claim that one result is correct.

## Concept 08 — Four variables, one cup

Akiko works with four practical variables around one cup: measured dry leaf on a small scale, a clear water carafe, a temperature-controlled kettle, and a simple timer. Keep one visual focal point and no labels. The scene should feel accessible, not laboratory-cold.

## Concept 09 — Water is an ingredient

Akiko compares the same tea brewed with three clear, unbranded water carafes and three matching cups. The liquids and cup volumes are plausible and consistent. No claim that one water is universally best; the image shows a careful home experiment.

## Concept 10 — The one-line brewing log

Akiko writes one concise observation in a plain field notebook beside a cup, dry leaf, scale, and kettle. Do not render legible writing. The final mood is calm continuity: a practice can be recorded, repeated, and improved without chasing perfection.
