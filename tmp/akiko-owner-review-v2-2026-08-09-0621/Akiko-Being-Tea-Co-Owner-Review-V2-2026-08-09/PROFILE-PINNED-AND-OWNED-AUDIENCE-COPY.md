**Preview:** Processing changes the path.

Most true tea begins with *Camellia sinensis*. Green, black, white, oolong, dark, and yellow tea are not six unrelated plants. Cultivar, place, season, processing, storage, and preparation all matter, but processing is one of the first useful distinctions.

This week, compare two teas from the same broad family or one tea brewed two ways. Record what you can observe; do not force a verdict.
