_Owner note (2026-08-10): "'soulular' a layer parallel to cellular."_

## Primary reading: a positioning idea / brand layer

**Soulular** = a play on **cellular**. Where the cellular network connects *devices*,
the **soulular** layer connects *identities / souls* — a parallel network for the many
sides of a person expressed as personas.

- **Cellular:** hardware, signal, the physical/carrier layer that moves data between phones.
- **Soulular:** the identity/expression layer AliaSpaces provides — each person runs many
  personas ("many sides of you"), each with its own voice, audience, and AI. The network
  isn't of devices but of *authored selves* and the people who follow them.

As positioning copy it writes itself: _"Cellular connects your phone. Soulular connects
your selves."_ / _"A parallel layer to cellular — a network for every side of you."_
It reinforces the existing tagline ("a home page for every side of you") and the
persona-as-identity model.
