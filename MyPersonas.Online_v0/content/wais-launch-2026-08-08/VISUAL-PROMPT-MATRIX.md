## Output contract

The preferred production standard is three separately generated versions of every numbered concept. They must share the same subject and narrative beat but must not be pixel-identical. Where the production variance above applies, use a platform-specific crop or zoom that remains visually distinct and preserve its provenance.

| Platform | Final size | Aspect | Composition rule |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Instagram | 1080 x 1350 JPG | 4:5 portrait | Close, vertically layered, strong mobile-feed silhouette; compatible with the current publishing API |
| Facebook | 1200 x 1500 JPG | 4:5 portrait | Slightly wider environmental context and a different camera angle |
| X | 1600 x 900 JPG | 16:9 landscape | Wide, immediate read with the focal subject inside the central safe area |

Instagram's native app now supports portrait photos through 3:4, but the current Instagram publishing API remains limited to 4:5 through 1.91:1. The 1080 x 1350 export therefore preserves strong portrait presence while remaining compatible with a future official MyPersonas publishing path. Facebook does not publish one universal organic-feed pixel recommendation; 1200 x 1500 is a deliberate mobile-feed production choice within Facebook's supported photo workflow. X's 16:9 choice sits inside X's officially supported full-display range for a single image.

## 01 — Signal Acquired

- Instagram: close frontal portrait; globe slightly above midpoint; halo nearly fills the vertical frame; one restrained aurora-green anomaly.
- Facebook: three-quarter orbital view; globe lower-left of center; halo sweeps through a deeper environmental field; quiet negative space above-right.
- X: low symmetrical horizon view; globe lower-center; halo extends toward both edges; strongest pulse just above the horizon.

## 02 — A Planet Is a Conversation

Base scene: atmospheric, oceanic, icy, terrestrial, and living patterns visibly exchange motion around WAIS as one coupled system, without becoming a labeled diagram.

- Instagram: near-polar view; cloud bands and ocean currents spiral vertically around the globe.
- Facebook: oblique equatorial view; atmospheric and ocean layers sweep from foreground left into the planet.
- X: distant three-quarter orbit; full globe on the left third; long coupled currents cross the wide field.

## 03 — Connection Is Infrastructure

Base scene: river deltas, roots, fungal branching, atmospheric currents, settlement lights, and star paths visually rhyme across scales in one coherent world.

- Instagram: macro branching forms enter from the lower foreground and resolve into the globe above.
- Facebook: river-like structures begin at lower left and transition naturally into the planetary horizon and halo.
- X: centered globe with several subtle branching layers radiating outward into deep space.

Do not create a collage, scientific diagram, or claim that every depicted relationship is literal.
