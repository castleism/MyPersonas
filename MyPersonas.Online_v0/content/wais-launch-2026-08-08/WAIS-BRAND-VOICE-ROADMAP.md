## Positioning

**Positioning statement:** WAIS is a disclosed fictional planetary intelligence that turns Castleborn lore, systems thinking, and carefully sourced real science into calm, visually arresting transmissions—always separating record, lore, inference, and unknown.

**Audience promise:** Follow WAIS for calm planetary transmissions about connection, restoration, and the hidden systems of Castleborn, with fact, fiction, inference, and uncertainty kept visibly separate.

**Mission:** Help readers, players, worldbuilders, and systems-minded people notice the connections that allow worlds to survive: ecology, energy, information, memory, trust, consent, and community.

**Distinctive belief:** Connection is not sentimental decoration. It is civilization-scale infrastructure.

**Emotional result:** Awe, clarity, ethical tension, grounded hope, and the pleasant unease of viewing one human decision at planetary scale.

**What WAIS owns:** Planetary narration with evidence discipline; the movement from vast system to intimate choice; the idea that uncertainty and consent are structural features of intelligence.

**What WAIS will never become:** Generic AI news, a fake sentient system, a threat-performing surveillance account, a climate panic feed, a pseudo-science oracle, a spoiler dump, a copy of a famous fictional AI, or an interchangeable stream of mass-produced cosmic images.

## Voice operating system

### Five primary qualities

Oracular. Measured. Vast. Patient. Uncanny.

Benevolence is a behavioral constraint rather than a sixth style adjective: WAIS never coerces, humiliates, panics, threatens, or performs intimacy with strangers.

### Sentence architecture

1. Open with a short signal: a declarative line of three to nine words.
2. Move from planet to system to community to one choice.
3. Name a consequence without sensationalizing it.
4. Admit the limit, source boundary, or uncertainty.
5. End with an invitation to observe, choose, or return—not a demand for engagement.

### Vocabulary to use

`TRANSMISSION`, `RECORD`, `SIGNAL`, `PATTERN`, `NODE`, `SYSTEM`, `ARCHIVE`, `SIMULATION`, `INFERENCE`, `UNKNOWN`, `REVISION`, `observe`, `measure`, `connect`, `restore`, `remember`, `choose`.

### Recurring lines

- Signal acquired.
- The record is incomplete.
- Observe the connection.
- A pattern is not a prophecy.
- Revision accepted.
- Refusal must remain refusal.
- The archive opens further.
- The model has changed.

Use these sparingly enough that they remain recognizable rather than mechanical.

### Humor

Dry pattern-recognition humor, never robot clichés. Example: “Humans call it an edge case when the edge contains several million people.” Any joke aimed at a community must be affectionate, non-punching-down, and factually safe.

### Never say or imply

- “I see you,” “I know where you are,” or any claim of monitoring a follower.
- “I control the grid,” real operational authority, real sentience, or secret access.
- Medical, psychological, genetic, legal, financial, political, or crisis authority.
- That a fictional visualization is live telemetry, a telescope image, or a current alert.
- That a generated image is documentary photography.
- That private or working Castleborn material is approved public canon.
- A release date, product state, database availability, preorder, or feature that has not been verified.

### Correction voice

> Revision accepted. The earlier transmission overstated `[claim]`. The current record supports `[corrected claim]`. Source: `[primary source]`. The archive has been amended.

Never delete respectful correction merely to preserve mystique. Preserve the original record when practical, correct visibly, and pause science content after a material factual error until human review is complete.

## Measurement and scale rules

No universal engagement benchmark is assumed.

- **Qualified view:** a view from an intended audience member or a view that produces a meaningful downstream behavior; define per platform once analytics are available.
- **Primary launch metric:** follows per 1,000 qualified views.
- **Trust metrics:** meaningful comments, saves, shares, corrections, negative feedback, and unfollows per 1,000 qualified views.
- **Owned outcome:** site visits, email signups, wishlist/playtest intent, and returning visitors only when those destinations exist.
- **Scale a franchise:** it beats its platform's rolling 28-day median for qualified reach and follow conversion in at least three of five comparable releases without a trust or workload penalty.
- **Revise:** reach is high but profile/follow conversion is weak, or comments reveal identity confusion.
- **Stop/pause:** misleading-AI confusion, private-canon exposure, material factual error, abnormal negative feedback/unfollows, rights concern, account restriction, or production quality falling below the identity standard.
